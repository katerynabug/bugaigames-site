Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 6, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains what information Faino (the "App") uses, why, and who it goes to. The App is made by Bugai Games, Lda., Portimão, Portugal ("we", "us", or "our"), and is available on the App Store and Google Play.

In short

  • No account, email or phone number. No ads.
  • One optional purchase, the Party Pass. Apple or Google takes the payment; we never see your card details.
  • Voice chat is live and is never recorded.
  • Rooms are private: only people who have your room code can join.
  • Anonymous usage statistics can be switched off in Settings.
  • We never sell your information.

2. Information We Do Not Collect

  • We do not ask for your real name. You can play under any name.
  • We do not collect your email address or phone number.
  • We do not collect your contacts, photos or files.
  • We do not collect your precise location.
  • We do not use an advertising ID, and the App shows no ads.
  • We do not collect payment details. Apple or Google handles Party Pass payments.

3. Microphone

The App uses your microphone only for voice chat while you are in a room. Your phone asks for your permission the first time. You can mute yourself at any time, and you can turn off microphone access in your phone's settings: the games still work, but the other players will not hear you. The App does not use the microphone on One Device (pass-the-phone) play.

4. Information Kept Only on Your Phone

Your name and colour, your language, the player names you typed for One Device play, your agreement to the Community rules, your usage statistics choice, your blocked players list, whether you own the Party Pass and which pass games you have tried for free are stored on your phone. Deleting the App deletes them. The Party Pass itself is not lost: you can restore it from your Apple or Google account.

5. Who We Share Information With

  • Unity Technologies runs the App's online services listed above (Authentication, Lobby, Relay, Vivox voice chat, Analytics, Cloud Diagnostics, Moderation and In-App Purchasing) on our behalf. See Unity's Privacy Policy.
  • Apple and Google take Party Pass payments and handle refunds under their own privacy policies.
  • The other players in your room see your name and colour, hear your voice and see what you type and draw in games. They also see whether the host has the Party Pass.
  • Authorities, only if the law requires us to.

We do not sell or rent your information, and we do not share it for advertising.

6. International Transfers

Unity may process information in the United States and in other countries outside your own. Where the law requires it, these transfers are protected by safeguards such as the European Commission's standard contractual clauses.

7. Legal Bases (European Economic Area and United Kingdom)

  • Rooms, names, voice chat and games: needed to provide the App you chose to use.
  • Reports, blocks, the word filter and removing players who break the rules: our legitimate interest in keeping players safe, and the rules of the app stores.
  • Party Pass purchases: needed to provide what you bought, and to meet our tax and accounting duties.
  • Crash reports: our legitimate interest in fixing the App.
  • Usage statistics: our legitimate interest in improving the App. You can object at any time by switching them off in Settings.

8. How Long We Keep Information

  • Room information (names, game moves, typed words and drawings) exists only while the room is open.
  • Voice is never stored.
  • Usage statistics are kept for up to 13 months.
  • Crash reports and player reports are kept only as long as needed to fix the problem or handle the report, within Unity's retention periods.
  • Purchase records are kept by Apple or Google under their own policies. We keep sales reports for as long as tax law requires.
  • Information on your phone stays until you delete the App.

9. Children

The App is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect information from children under 13. If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has used the App's online features, please contact us at support@bugaigames.com and we will take steps to delete their information.

10. Your Rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to:

  • Ask what information we hold about you, and get a copy.
  • Ask us to correct or delete it.
  • Object to or limit how we use it.
  • Complain to your data protection authority (for example the CNPD in Portugal, or SDAIA in Saudi Arabia).

These rights come from laws such as the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and Saudi Arabia's Personal Data Protection Law (PDPL). Because the App does not know who you are, we can only find information linked to your anonymous player ID. You can find your player ID in the App under Settings, where a tap copies it. Include it when you email us at support@bugaigames.com and we will help. The ID appears after you first create or join a room; until then the App has no player ID, so there is nothing we can find for you. You can also switch off usage statistics in Settings at any time, and deleting the App removes everything stored on your phone (the App creates a new player ID if you install it again).

11. Data Security

We use Unity's online services and take reasonable steps to protect your information. However, no method of sending or storing information over the internet is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

12. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will post the new version on this page and update the "Last updated" date. If a change affects how the App uses your information in an important way, we will also tell you in the App.

13. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us. See also our Terms of Use.
