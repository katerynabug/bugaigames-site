Privacy Policy

Last updated: April 15, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how Bugai Games ("we", "us", or "our") collects, uses, and shares information when you use Higher Lower: Trivia Edition (the "App"), available on Google Play.

By using the App, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this policy.

1. Information We Collect

1.1 Information collected automatically

Data TypePurposeCollected By
Device type, OS versionApp compatibility and crash reportingFirebase / Google Play
Advertising ID (GAID)Serving relevant adsGoogle AdMob
App usage data (levels played, features used)Analytics to improve the gameFirebase Analytics
Crash logsIdentifying and fixing bugsFirebase Crashlytics
Purchase historyProcessing in-app purchasesGoogle Play Billing

1.2 Information we do NOT collect

  • We do not collect your name, email address, or phone number.
  • We do not collect precise location data.
  • We do not collect photos, contacts, or files from your device.
  • We do not require account creation to play the game.

1.3 Game progress data

Your game progress (level completion, coins, stickers, battle pass progress) is stored locally on your device. We do not upload or store your game save data on our servers.

2. How We Use Information

  • To provide and maintain the App — ensuring it works correctly on your device.
  • To display advertisements — we use Google AdMob to show banner, interstitial, and rewarded video ads. AdMob may use your Advertising ID to personalize ads.
  • To process purchases — when you make in-app purchases, Google Play handles the transaction. We receive a purchase confirmation but not your payment details.
  • To improve the App — we use aggregated, anonymous analytics data to understand which features are popular and identify bugs.

3. Third-Party Services

The App uses the following third-party services, each with their own privacy policies:

  • Google Play Services
  • Google AdMob
  • Firebase Analytics & Crashlytics

4. Advertising

The App displays ads provided by Google AdMob. You may see personalized ads based on your interests or non-personalized ads based on contextual information.

You can opt out of personalized advertising by adjusting your device settings:

  • Android: Settings → Google → Ads → Opt out of Ads Personalization

If you purchase the "Remove Ads" option within the App, banner and interstitial ads will no longer be displayed. Rewarded video ads (which you choose to watch voluntarily) may still be available.

5. In-App Purchases

The App offers optional in-app purchases (coin packs, ad removal, premium features). All transactions are processed by Google Play. We do not have access to your payment information (credit card number, billing address, etc.). Please refer to Google's Privacy Policy for details on how payment data is handled.

6. Children's Privacy

The App is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has provided us with personal information, please contact us at support@bugaigames.com and we will take steps to delete such information.

7. Data Retention

Analytics data is retained for up to 14 months in aggregate form. Crash reports are retained for up to 90 days. Game progress is stored only on your device and is deleted when you uninstall the App or clear its data.

8. Data Security

We value your trust in providing us with your information and strive to use commercially acceptable means of protecting it. However, no method of transmission over the internet or electronic storage is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

9. Your Rights

Depending on your jurisdiction, you may have the right to:

  • Request access to data collected about you.
  • Request deletion of your data.
  • Opt out of personalized advertising (see Section 4).
  • Withdraw consent at any time by uninstalling the App.

If you are in the European Economic Area (EEA), you have rights under the GDPR. To exercise these rights, contact us at support@bugaigames.com.

10. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page and updating the "Last updated" date. You are advised to review this page periodically.
